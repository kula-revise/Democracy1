The essay so far

Neither side could win, but a stalemate does not negotiate itself: leaders had to choose to act on it, and in 1990 De Klerk and Mandela did.

That is sentence one. It concedes something real, that both sides were stuck, and then it refuses to let the stalemate do the work on its own. Hold that shape. Every sentence you add over the next four lessons keeps conceding and then judging.
